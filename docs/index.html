Moves
    📜 Rules
    • Goal: be first to land on any square of the far row.
    • Each turn, exactly one action: move your pawn one square up/down/left/right — or place one wall. No passing.
    • A wall is 2 squares long, sits in the grooves (horizontal/vertical), blocks both pawns, and is permanent. You have 10.
    • Walls cannot overlap or cross, and a placement is illegal if it leaves either pawn with no route to its goal (checked by BFS).
    • Face-to-face: jump straight over your opponent. If a wall or the edge is behind them, step diagonally to either side.
    • Win by reaching the far row, or if your opponent resigns or runs out of time.
    An original implementation of the Barricade.gg game concept — Vite + TypeScript, hosted on GitHub Pages with Firebase online play. Not affiliated with barricade.gg.
